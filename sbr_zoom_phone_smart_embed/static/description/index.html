How It Works

Seamless calling from any record

The module embeds Zoom Phone as an iframe widget and wires it into Odoo's phone fields and chatter.

When you click a phone number field on any Odoo record, the module intercepts the default browser dial action and routes it to the embedded Zoom Phone Smart Embed iframe in your systray bubble. The current record's model and ID are captured at that moment — so when the call ends, the call log is posted to the correct record's chatter automatically.

Example — Calling from a Contact record
1 Click the phone number field on a Contact record
Zoom Phone bubble opens, number is pre-filled
2 If Auto Dial is on — call is placed immediately; otherwise confirm manually
Conversation happens in Zoom Phone
3 Call ends — Odoo receives the call-ended event from the Zoom iframe
Record was already bound at step 1
4 Call log is posted automatically to the Contact's chatter
Chatter entry — what the call log looks like
✅ Completed — John Smith called Alice Cooper on 17 Feb 2026, 10:42 AM
❌ Missed — +1 555 0199 called Sales on 17 Feb 2026, 09:15 AM
🚫 Rejected — Bob Miller called Support on 16 Feb 2026, 03:30 PM
💡 The floating bubble can be dragged to any position on screen and stays fully within the viewport, including after window resize.
Configuration

Set up in two steps

Configure Zoom's allowed origins, then flip a couple of toggles in Odoo Settings.

1
Whitelist your Odoo domain in the Zoom Developer Console
Required before the Smart Embed iframe will load

Log in to the Zoom App Marketplace and open your Smart Embed application. Under Allowed Domains / Origins, add your Odoo instance's base URL (e.g., https://your-odoo.example.com). Save and publish. Without this, the iframe will be blocked by Zoom's security policy.

2
Configure call behaviour in Odoo Settings
Settings → General Settings → Zoom Phone section

Navigate to Settings → General Settings and scroll to the Zoom Phone block. Two toggles are available:

⚡ Enable Auto Dial
When ON — clicking a phone number places the call immediately. When OFF — the dialer is pre-filled and the user dials manually.
🔔 Enable Notification
When ON — Odoo shows in-app notification toasts for call events directly in the interface.
3
Call from any phone field
Works on Contacts, Leads, Customers, and any record with a phone field

Open any record with a phone field. Click the number to open the Zoom dialer pre-filled, or click the SMS icon to compose a Zoom SMS. The Zoom Phone bubble in the systray can also be toggled manually at any time and dragged freely across the screen.

4
Review auto-logged call activity on the record
Calls are posted to chatter automatically after the call ends

After a call completes, a formatted entry is posted to the record's chatter showing call status (Completed ✅ / Missed ❌ / Rejected 🚫), caller and callee names or numbers, and the exact timestamp. No manual note-taking required.
