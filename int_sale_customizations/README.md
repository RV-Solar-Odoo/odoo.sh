# Sales Customizations

My Orders lists carrier tracking for every outgoing shipment. My Orders has four tabs: All Orders (search, filters, export), Favorite Orders, Backorders (orders with an open backorder), and Quotes (Odoo's quotations, `/my/quotes`; the Quotations box is gone from My Portal). Backordered orders carry a Backordered pill on All Orders. Dealers see outgoing shipments only, never warehouse picks. The order page links each shipment to a page with the ship-to, the products, and the delivery slip. `/my/deliveries` and `/my/backorders` open My Orders.

The order page uses Odoo's delivery list for the status and the return slip. Sale Information stays Sale Information.

Each shipped delivery with a tracking number is a link when the carrier has a URL, and plain text when it does not. Several numbers on one delivery are listed separately. An order with nothing shipped shows —. An order with some deliveries still open shows those links plus Partial. Cancelled shipments are skipped. The links are read from the order's pickings in one pass.

My Orders also searches by order number, PO, ship-to, product, or serial, and filters by date and status (All, Open, Shipped, Cancelled, Cancellation requested). All includes cancelled orders. Pins, reorder, export, and PO upload follow the portal role. The export has one row per physical product, with the delivery method as a column; shipping, discount and coupon lines, down payments, and notes are not rows. Reorder skips the same charge lines. A cancellation request does not cancel or refund the order. A sales manager confirms or denies it from the order. Store credit needs a program and a top-up product under Settings, and an accountant should review that before production.

## Customer quotes

Owners and buyers write quotes for the dealer's own customers at `/my/customer-quotes`, to download or print and send themselves; quotes are never emailed from the portal (SPEC decision 44). The My Orders Quotes tab is still quotations from RV Solar. Customer Quotes has two tabs: **Quotes** and, for the account owner, **Quote Settings** (SPEC decision 45).

- **Customer price.** A quote is priced at MAP or MSRP, what the retail customer pays the dealer. New Quote starts at the Quote Settings default and can pick the other one; the editor has no MAP/MSRP switch, so a customer looking at the screen doesn't see it. A line with no MAP uses MSRP. The dealer can type any price on a line; below MAP still saves.
- **Editor.** Customer details fold into one row that opens to edit (the address list is the dealer's saved Customer Addresses). Lines look like Edit Favorite (picture, name, SKU, price each, − / + quantity, total, bin) and reuse its row styles and `favorites.js` stepper and bin; the price box and stepper are the same height. Labor and other charges open under the lines. A totals card (Products, Labor and charges, Customer total) sits beside the memo, and a sticky bar at the bottom holds the total, Download PDF, Print and Save Quote. Totals follow quantity and price as they change (`quotes.js`); Save Quote stores them.
- **Show Dealer Info** (off each time the page opens) reveals wholesale, margin, the margin total and the MAP notes. Wholesale is the dealer's pricelist and never reaches the product search.
- **Quote Settings** (`/my/customer-quotes/settings`, Owner): default price, logo (the company contact's `image_1920`, the same image My Portal → Documents shows read-only), company subheading, accent colour (six swatches or a custom colour) and a footer line, with a live preview. Stored on the commercial partner as `int_quote_price_basis`, `int_quote_accent`, `int_quote_subheading`, `int_quote_footer`; staff can set them on the contact.
- **PDF** (`/my/customer-quotes/<id>/pdf`, `?download=1` to save): report `action_report_int_dealer_quote` on its own Letter paper format. Built on `web.html_container`, not `web.external_layout`, so nothing of RV Solar's prints, and never wholesale or margin. Black and white plus the accent on the top band, headings and total; text in the accent is darkened when the colour is too light to read. Logo, name and subheading; From (with "Prepared by", the user who made the quote) and Prepared for; lines with pictures; notes; totals before sales tax; the footer line and page numbers on every page. It shows the last saved version. Laid out with tables, as wkhtmltopdf does not lay out flex or grid.

## Checkout: pay for what ships now

Depends on Victron Stock (backorder lines) and Shippo Customizations (shipping quotes).

- **Payment step.** The cart's backordered quantity is split onto Backorder lines (`int_victron_stock`). The order summary (`total_backorder_due_today` on `website_sale.total`) has two parts. **Paying today**: items shipping now, their shipping (Free at $0) and their tax, adding up to Due Today. **Billed when it ships** (shaded): the backordered items, shipping TBD and their tax, with one line saying we'll bill them once there's an ETA. Figures come from `sale.order._int_checkout_summary`; a tax row is left out when its tax is 0, as for a tax-exempt dealer. Odoo's Delivery, Subtotal, Taxes and Total rows stay in the page, hidden, because the checkout script updates them. On phones every step after the cart shows the summary on the page, under the step's content, instead of in a side panel (`checkout_layout_due_today`); a sticky bar at the bottom keeps the Pay / Continue button. The button reads "Pay $X Now". The card form charges the pay-now amount, and `/shop/payment/transaction/<id>` accepts that amount for these carts. Express checkout is off for them. The thank-you page compares the payment with the pay-now amount (`sale.order._int_payment_matches`), not the full total.
- **All-backorder carts** get the $0 "Ships When Backorder Arrives" method from Shippo Customizations instead of a parcel quote.
- **Why no Pay on Account.** When a logged-in customer isn't offered it, the server log has a line starting `No Pay on Account for`, naming the check that stopped it, the contact the terms were read from, and the terms and limit for each company.
- **Place Backorder.** A cart that is all backorder has nothing to pay. Odoo's Confirm Order button is relabelled and `/shop/payment/validate` confirms it.
- **Place Order on Account** (`/shop/int_on_account`). Shown to a dealer login that can order when the company contact has payment terms other than Immediate. It works when there is a credit limit, no overdue invoice, and enough available credit for the pay-now amount (`res.partner._int_terms_status` in Accounting Customizations). Otherwise the card says why. Buyers can use it but only Owners and Accountants see the credit figure. The order gets the dealer's terms and the Placed on Account badge. Staff invoice it as usual.
- **Coupons** only count and discount what is paid now: open Backorder lines are added to `_get_no_effect_on_threshold_lines`. That needs `sale_loyalty`, now a dependency.
- **Shipping quotes** leave out backordered units (`sale.order.line._int_shipping_quote_qty`), and so does Shippo's free-shipping threshold.
- **Cancel Backordered Items** on the order page cancels only the backorder. It is immediate while its PO is a draft. Once the PO is confirmed, or the lines were released for invoicing, it is a cancellation request (`int_cancel_scope = backorder`) that a sales manager confirms from the order. If the lines were already invoiced, the manager issues the credit note.

## Decisions

Favorite orders (`int.favorite.order`) are saved product lists shared by every login of the dealer (the commercial partner) on that company's website, 25 per dealer by default (`int_max_favorites` in Settings). Starring an order on All Orders or the order page saves its product lines (no shipping, discounts, notes) under the order number, renamed in the same dialog. "Save Cart as a Favorite" on the cart saves a list that was never ordered. A favorite is a copy: editing it (name, quantities, remove, add a product by name or SKU) never touches the order. Owner and Buyer change favorites; everyone who sees orders sees them; adding to the cart follows the ordering role and uses today's prices. Removing one archives it so Undo can bring it back. The 19.0.1.4.0 update turned each starred order into a favorite named by its order number. Reorder explains unavailable products before adding anything. A spreadsheet PO goes into the cart. A PDF PO becomes a draft quotation with a private attachment. The ship-to dock, forklift, and location type are copied onto the delivery when it is created.
